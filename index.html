Got Questions?

FAQ

We primarily operate by appointment to ensure every client gets our full, undivided attention. However, we do accommodate walk-ins based on availability. To guarantee your preferred time slot, we strongly recommend booking in advance — especially on weekends.

Color appointments, especially complex services like balayage, full color correction, or creative color, should be booked 1–2 weeks in advance. These services require dedicated time and a pre-consultation. Contact us to schedule your consultation today.

We require 24-hour notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a booking fee. We respect our stylists' time and ask that you respect it too — your appointment slot is held exclusively for you.

Absolutely. For major transformations — color corrections, dramatic cuts, or first-time complex color — we highly recommend a free consultation first. This allows us to assess your hair health, understand your vision, and create a personalised plan for your best possible result.

We exclusively partner with premium, professional-grade brands including Matrix, Schwarzkopf Professional, Wella Professionals, and Olaplex. We also stock retail products for home maintenance so you can extend the life of your salon results.

After every service, your stylist will walk you through a personalised home care routine — from the right products to washing frequency and heat styling tips. We believe your hair should look amazing on day 30, not just day 1.
